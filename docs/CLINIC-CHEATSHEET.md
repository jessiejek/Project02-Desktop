# Clinic Node: one-page cheat sheet (Grace Medical Clinic)

Print this page. Keep it at the front desk.

## Every morning
1. Open **Clinic Node** and sign in with **your own email and password**.
2. The top bar shows **Online** (green dot) or **Offline mode**. The first sign-in of the day syncs by itself: wait for the results screen, then press **Continue**.
3. Never share your password. Never leave the screen signed in when you walk away.

## What the top bar means
| You see | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Online** + a time (e.g. 250 ms) | Connected to the clinic server | Work normally |
| **Slow** / **Unstable** | The internet is weak | Keep working. If saves fail, ask the doctor to switch to **Offline mode** |
| **Offline mode** | Everything is saved on THIS computer and sent later | Work normally. Press **Sync now** when the internet is back |
| **N changes waiting** | Saved here, not on the server yet | Press **Sync now**, or just sign out / quit (it syncs first) |

## Front desk (Staff)
- **Walk-in:** Walk-in → find or register the patient → pick the doctor → the queue number (R-001 for regular, S-001 for senior/PWD/pregnant) prints on the slip.
- **Payment:** Payments → open the visit → choose the method → type the exact amount → **Confirm**. In Offline mode, write the **booklet receipt number** and type it in.
- **Wrong patient or amount?** Do not delete. Tell the admin.

## Doctor
- **Today** shows who is waiting. Press **Resume / Start** on a patient. Follow the steps on the left: Clinical notes → Vitals → Prescription → Labs → Follow-up → Medical certificate → Vaccines → Professional fee.
- **Save & next** saves and moves on (a spinner shows while it saves). **Review & complete** finishes the visit.
- **Print** opens the normal Mac / Windows print window. Choose the printer and press Print.
- **Profile** lets you change your name, specialization, fee, license, PTR, S2, bio, email and password.

## Admin
- **Sync computers:** see every computer and when it last synced. Revoke a lost one.
- **Sync review:** the computer found two patients that may be the same person. Open it, compare, then **merge** or **not a duplicate**.
- **Staff / Doctors:** invite or edit people; change an email in the person's profile.
- **My account:** change your own password and email.

## When something goes wrong
| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Could not save" | Press the button once more. If it repeats, switch to **Offline mode** (doctor or admin) and tell the admin |
| Page is blank or stuck | Wait 5 seconds, then press **Try again**. Still stuck? Sign out and in |
| Forgot password | Ask the admin to reset it (email reset is not set up yet) |
| Wrong queue number or duplicate receipt | Admin → **Receipt clashes** / **Sync review** |
| Power cut or computer crash | Open the app again. Nothing saved is lost. Press **Sync now** |

## Rules that protect the patients
- Sign out when you leave the desk.
- Only the doctor may switch **Offline mode** on or off.
- Do not take screenshots of patient records.
- Report anything strange to the admin straight away.

*Support: the clinic admin. Server address and logins are kept by the owner, never on paper at the desk.*
